

# 🧬 ISO 20387 Biobanking Accreditation Roadmap

This section tracks our strategic timeline and readiness toward achieving **ISO 20387** accreditation for the biobank. Our ultimate objective is to secure our status as the **6th accredited biobank in the United Kingdom**.

---

## 📅 Key Milestones & Timeline

* **July 2026:** Official Application Submission.
* **Late July / August 2026:** UKAS Initial Review Window (Expected 2-week turnaround response).
* **October 2026:** Target window for the **Pre-Assessment** audit.
* **June 2027:** Target window for the **Full Accreditation Assessment**.

---

## 🔍 Strategic Context & Risk Log

* **Assessor Bottleneck:** UKAS currently has only **1 active assessor** qualified for ISO 20387. This represents a critical timeline risk; early submission and rigid adherence to the October pre-assessment window are vital to maintaining our slot.
* **Competitive Landscape:** There are currently **4 accredited biobanks** in the UK. A 5th biobank is expected to achieve accreditation within the next couple of months. Efficient execution of this roadmap positions our site to successfully debut as the **6th** accredited facility nationwide.

---

## ✏️ Keeping Data Updated
When milestones progress, open `data.json` and adjust the properties:
1. Bump the `progress` percentage (0 to 100) to update the dashboard bars and auto-calculate the overall readiness score.
2. Update the `phaseText` to reflect current states (e.g., changing `"AWAITING SUBMISSION"` to `"IN PROGRESS"`).
